Scoring Methodology

How quantitative and qualitative data combine into a final score

The AI report combines quantitative (financial) and qualitative (lifestyle) data using your importance weights to produce a holistic family score per city.

Formula

40% | 60%

City Score = 0.4 × Quantitative + 0.6 × Qualitative + Personal Fit

Quantitative Score — based on net savings, savings rate, and income-to-expense ratio. Cities with negative savings are capped at 5/10.

Qualitative Score — weighted average of all parameters:

Score = Σ(ratingi × importancei) / Σ(importancei)

where rating maps: ✅ Top = 10, ⚠️ Medium = 6, ➖ Low = 3, ❌ Critical = 1

and importancei is your weight (1–5) for each parameter

Personal Fit — ±1 adjustment per member based on traits, priorities, and hobbies.

💡 Tip: Set importance to 5 for deal-breaker factors and 1 for nice-to-haves. Drag the slider to adjust how much financial vs lifestyle factors matter.

The AI uses this framework as a guideline. Final scores also incorporate cross-parameter interactions and long-term trajectory analysis.
